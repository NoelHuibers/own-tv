# Remove optional LG data while keeping the account

Local privacy settings and domain blocking do not erase records LG already has.
Use LG's official account/TV controls and privacy request process for that.

## On the TV

Under Settings → Support → Privacy & Terms → Delete My Personal Information,
look for Delete Viewing Information and Delete Voice Information. Names and
availability vary by region and firmware. These actions require a network
connection and may require signing in. Read the result on the TV: do not assume
that withdrawing optional consent also removed historical records.

If an existing blocklist prevents a deletion request reaching LG, use only a
narrow temporary exception for the actual privacy endpoint and restore it after
submission. Do not re-enable ACR, accept new optional tracking agreements or
unblock all LG services merely to make a button work.

Reset advertising ID and clear advertising cookies separately. A successful reset
response alone does not prove a new advertising ID was assigned. Record only
whether the value changed, never publish either value.

## LG-held data

Open https://privacy.lg.com/ and use My Data after signing in with the LG account
linked to your television. Request deletion of optional TV viewing, voice,
advertising, profiling, marketing and diagnostic data while retaining the account.
If that portal does not cover Smart TV records, LG's TV membership privacy policy
lists `tv.privacy@lge.com` and, for European requests, `dpo-eu@lge.com`.

Suggested request (fill account identifiers privately, not in Git):

> I request deletion of personal data associated with my LG Smart TV and LG
> account that is not necessary to maintain my account or provide services I
> explicitly use. This includes viewing/ACR history, voice-related data,
> advertising identifiers and profiles, marketing and cross-device profiles,
> diagnostics and optional usage analytics. I withdraw consent for these optional
> purposes and object to direct-marketing profiling. Please retain my LG account,
> purchases and essential account services. Please confirm the data categories
> erased, any recipients notified, and any categories retained with the reason and
> retention period. Please tell me a secure way to verify my identity if needed.

Keep the submission receipt and response privately. A submitted request is not a
confirmation that deletion has happened. Third-party streaming accounts retain
separate histories and privacy controls.
